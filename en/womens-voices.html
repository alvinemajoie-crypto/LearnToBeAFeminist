Women’s voices

First-person stories, told in the words of those who live them.

Why this section?

Statistics show the scale of inequality. Testimonies show what it changes in a life. This section gives the floor to African women: their journeys, their obstacles, their victories.

Coming soon. The first testimonies will be published here, added one by one.

Our principles

  • Consent. No testimony is published without the person’s written agreement.
  • Their own words. We proofread and format, without changing the meaning.
  • Anonymity is possible. Each woman chooses to appear under her full name, a first name, or anonymously.
  • Safety first. We do not publish details that could put a person at risk.

Share your story
