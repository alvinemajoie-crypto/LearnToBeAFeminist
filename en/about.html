About

An independent, bilingual magazine to learn, share and transform.

Our mission

LearnToBeAFeminist is a digital magazine on feminist education, women’s rights, gender inequality and the experiences of African women. Our ambition: to make these subjects accessible, rigorous and alive.

What you will find here

  • clear articles on education, rights, work and history;
  • landmarks to understand feminism;
  • stories in the Women’s voices section.

Why two languages?

French and English are both working and reading languages across the continent. Every page exists in both languages, to reach more readers.
