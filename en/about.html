Welcome to LearnToBeAFeminist

I am Alvine Doumbia, a feminist for four years. Born into an African family and raised in an environment deeply attached to Christian values, I learned to question traditions, social norms, and the place assigned to women.

In Africa, feminism still meets resistance, sometimes because it is perceived as alien to our realities. Yet, do African women have fewer reasons to demand equality, simply because their struggles differ from those of women elsewhere?

Our experiences are multiple, shaped by gender, social class, religion, origin, and many other realities. That is why it is essential to understand feminism in all its intersectionality, without reducing women’s experiences to a single narrative.

Whether you are passionate about feminism, simply curious, or fiercely opposed to its ideas, you are welcome here. Let us learn, question, and understand together.
